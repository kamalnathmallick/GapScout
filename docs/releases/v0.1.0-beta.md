# GapScout v0.1.0-beta

**Tag:** `v0.1.0-beta`  
**Date:** November 20, 2026  
**Status:** Pre-release / Beta

---

## Highlights

GapScout v0.1.0-beta introduces the core spatial market intelligence engine, interactive deck.gl map, 20-feature explainable lead scorer, and LLM-powered storefront and review enrichment with strict SSRF defense and cost caps.

### 🗺️ Interactive H3 Hex Gap Map
- **MapLibre GL + deck.gl Overlay:** High-performance H3 hexagonal rendering over OpenFreeMap liberty vector tiles.
- **Color-Blind-Safe Visualization:** Diverging emerald-to-red palette across Opportunity, Supply, Density, Rating, and Demand metrics.
- **Interactive Inspection:** Side drawer detailing explainable gap factors and underlying business directories.
- **Summary Cards & Export:** Top 3 opportunity and saturated areas with smooth fly-to animations; one-click PNG snapshot and CSV dataset download.

### ⚡ Resumable Scraper Orchestration & Cleaning
- **Grid Planner:** Decomposes geographic bounding boxes into overlapping search cells with automated cost/time estimation.
- **Celery Task Pipeline:** Distributed, fault-tolerant cell scraping with exponential backoff retries.
- **Data Normalization:** Maps messy categories into a controlled taxonomy; normalizes phones to E.164; validates domain MX records; performs 50m spatial duplicate merging.

### 🎯 Explainable Lead Scoring Engine
- **20 Versioned Features (`v1`):** Extracts deterministic place metrics, review sentiment, owner response rates, and neighborhood gap scores.
- **5-Part Cold-Start Scoring:** Evaluates Pain (max 25), Fit (max 25), Timing (max 15), Reachability (max 20), and Evidence (max 15).
- **Offer Profiles:** Workspace settings (target categories, rating range, geographic regions) directly controlling Fit points.
- **Transparent Breakdowns:** Structured JSON rationale explaining points awarded for every lead.

### 🤖 LLM Enrichment with Cost Caps
- **Strict SSRF Defense:** Blocks private, loopback, link-local, multicast, and cloud metadata IP ranges.
- **Deterministic Signals:** SSL validation, mobile viewport tags, contact forms, booking links, and social presence.
- **Claude Storefront & Review Analysis:** Extracts value proposition clarity, digital maturity scores, recurring complaint themes, and one-line pitch hooks.
- **Budget Protection:** SHA-256 content-hash caching prevents redundant token spending; daily workspace dollar caps prevent cost overruns.
